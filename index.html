Grievance Redressal Mechanism & Guidelines

1. Complaint Categories Supported

Students can lodge complaints across: Academic Concerns, Hostel & Mess, Library & Digital Access, Infrastructure & Labs, Transportation, and General Administration.

2. Resolution Timeline

All grievances are acknowledged instantly with a unique Complaint ID. Departmental reviewers are assigned within 24 hours, with target resolution within 3 business days.

3. Confidentiality & Security

All submissions are strictly tracked against the student's Roll Number and institutional email, ensuring authenticity and zero retaliation policy.

4. Status Definitions

Pending Awaiting verification • In Progress Department assigned • Resolved Rectified and closed.
